# Intents

## Overview

`purge/Intents` is Purge's external-action surface. Three App Intents — `UninstallAppIntent`, `ScanForJunkIntent`, and `CleanSafeJunkIntent` — expose uninstall review, junk scanning, and safe cleaning to Siri, Spotlight, and App Shortcuts. `PurgeAppShortcuts` owns the registered phrases and shortcut metadata, while `IntentRouter` is the `@MainActor` façade that turns an intent into store work, window state, and a one-line answer.

The same routing layer serves Finder: `FinderUninstallService` is the `NSApp.servicesProvider` target for the "Uninstall with Purge" service, and `ExternalUninstallHandler` also receives app bundles dropped on the Dock icon. Both resolve the selection through `ExternalUninstallRequest` and end at `IntentRouter.showUninstaller`, so Spotlight and Finder share one review path. The intents themselves do not delete anything; they open the window and/or drive `PurgeStore`, and the review sheet remains the confirmation point for uninstalls.

## Interface

**App Intents and answers**

- `UninstallAppIntent` (`UninstallAppIntent.swift:UninstallAppIntent`): `title`, `description`, `openAppWhenRun = true`, parameter `app: InstalledAppEntity`, `parameterSummary`, and `perform()`. `perform()` starts an un-awaited `Task` calling `IntentRouter.shared.showUninstaller(appID:name:)` so Spotlight is not blocked on loading the app list.
- `ScanForJunkIntent` (`ScanForJunkIntent.swift:ScanForJunkIntent`): `title`, `description` with search keywords, `openAppWhenRun = true`, and `perform()`, which awaits `IntentRouter.shared.scanMac()` and returns `styledAnswer` plus `dialog`.
- `ScanForJunkIntent.swift:IntentRouter.ScanOutcome.styledAnswer` and `ScanForJunkIntent.swift:IntentRouter.ScanOutcome.dialog`: map `.needsSetup`, `.busyCleaning`, `.scanned(bytes)`, and `.stopped` to localized text; a positive size is bolded with `IntentAnswer.bolding`.
- `CleanSafeJunkIntent` (`CleanSafeJunkIntent.swift:CleanSafeJunkIntent`): `title`, `description` with cleaning keywords, `openAppWhenRun = true`, and `perform()`, which reads `NSWorkspace.shared.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion` and awaits `IntentRouter.shared.cleanSafeJunk(reduceMotion:)`.
- `CleanSafeJunkIntent.swift:IntentRouter.CleanOutcome.styledAnswer`, `CleanSafeJunkIntent.swift:IntentRouter.CleanOutcome.dialog`, and `CleanSafeJunkIntent.swift:IntentRouter.CleanOutcome.failureNote`: map `.needsSetup`, `.busyCleaning`, `.nothingToClean`, `.stopped`, and `.cleaned(bytes:failedCount:)` to localized text.
- `IntentAnswer.bolding(_:in:)` (`IntentAnswer.swift:IntentAnswer.bolding(_:in:)`): finds the formatted byte count in the answer and applies `.stronglyEmphasized`; returns plain text when no size is present.

**Shortcuts registration**

- `PurgeAppShortcuts` (`PurgeAppShortcuts.swift:PurgeAppShortcuts`) is a `nonisolated struct` conforming to `AppShortcutsProvider`; `appShortcuts` declares one `AppShortcut` each for `ScanForJunkIntent`, `CleanSafeJunkIntent`, and `UninstallAppIntent`, with phrases, `shortTitle`, and `systemImageName`.
- `PurgeAppShortcuts.refreshAppList()` wraps `updateAppShortcutParameters()` so callers that do not import AppIntents can re-read the installed apps used by the uninstall phrases.

**Router**

- `IntentRouter` (`IntentRouter.swift:IntentRouter`) is `@MainActor final class` with `shared` built from `AppEnvironment.store`, `IntentRouter.revealWindow`, `FirstRunGate.hasCompletedOnboarding`, and `ExternalUninstallHandler.showRefusal`; `init(store:reveal:onboardingDone:reviewUninstall:showRefusal:)` allows tests to inject a fake store, disable disk-walking review construction, and suppress the refusal alert.
- `IntentRouter.revealWindow()` calls `RemovedAppMonitor.shared.userOpenedWindow()` and `AppWindowPresenter.reveal()`.
- `IntentRouter.ScanOutcome` (`IntentRouter.swift:IntentRouter.ScanOutcome`) with cases `needsSetup`, `busyCleaning`, `scanned(safeBytes:)`, `stopped`, and `IntentRouter.swift:scanMac()` — selects `.overview`, reveals the window, checks onboarding and deletion state, joins or starts a cache/dev-tools scan, waits for it, and reports `store.safeRecoverableBytes`.
- `IntentRouter.CleanOutcome` (`IntentRouter.swift:IntentRouter.CleanOutcome`) with cases `needsSetup`, `busyCleaning`, `nothingToClean`, `stopped`, `cleaned(bytes:failedCount:)`, and `IntentRouter.swift:cleanSafeJunk(reduceMotion:now:)` — scans only when there are no fresh cache results, waits for any running cache scan, re-checks deletion/cleanup state, then calls `store.cleanSafeItemsFromOverview(reduceMotion:)` and awaits its summary.
- `IntentRouter.swift:showUninstaller(appID:name:)` — selects the `.uninstaller` tab and `.installedApps` section, replaces `selectedAppIDs` with the one app, sets `uninstallerFocus`, reveals the window, refreshes permissions, waits for the app list, retries one app scan when the app is on disk but unlisted, and opens the review only when the app is both listed and still on disk.
- `IntentRouter.UninstallMiss` (`IntentRouter.swift:IntentRouter.UninstallMiss`) with cases `gone(name:)` and `notListed(name:)`, plus `IntentRouter.swift:isStillWaiting(on:)`, guard the review against a changed selection or an already-open plan.
- Private helpers: `IntentRouter.swift:requestUninstallReview()`, `IntentRouter.swift:waitForCacheScan()`, `IntentRouter.swift:hasFreshCacheResults(now:)`, and `IntentRouter.swift:appIsListed(_:)`.

**Entities and the app index**

- `InstalledAppEntity` (`InstalledAppEntity.swift:InstalledAppEntity`) is an `AppEntity`; `id` is the standardized bundle path (matching `InstalledApp.id`), with `name`, `bundleID`, `iconPNG`, `init(_:iconPNG:)`, `displayRepresentation`, `typeDisplayRepresentation`, and `defaultQuery`.
- `InstalledAppQuery` (`InstalledAppEntity.swift:InstalledAppQuery`) is an `EntityStringQuery` whose `@concurrent` methods are `entities(for:)`, `entities(matching:)`, and `suggestedEntities()`.
- `InstalledAppIndex` (`InstalledAppEntity.swift:InstalledAppIndex`) is an `actor` cache with a 30-second list lifetime, a path-keyed icon cache, `apps(now:)`, `icon(for:)`, `iconPNG(atPath:side:)` (64 px by default), and `matching(_:in:)`. `iconPNG` draws through `NSWorkspace.shared.icon(forFile:)` into an sRGB bitmap at the requested side length.

**Finder, Services, and Dock routing**

- `ExternalUninstallHandler` (`FinderUninstallService.swift:ExternalUninstallHandler`) is `@MainActor`; `handle(urls:)` resolves the selection on a detached user-initiated task and drops results that a newer request has superseded; `show(_:canAddFolder:urls:)`, `offerToAddFolder(_:title:detail:urls:)`, and `showRefusal(title:detail:)` present the outcomes. `latestRequest` is the monotonically increasing request counter.
- `FinderUninstallService` (`FinderUninstallService.swift:FinderUninstallService`) is the `NSObject` service target. `menuTitle = "Uninstall with Purge"` and `message = "uninstallWithPurge"` must match the `NSMenuItem` title and `NSMessage` in `Info.plist`; `uninstallWithPurge(_:userData:error:)` reads file URLs from the pasteboard and forwards to `ExternalUninstallHandler.handle(urls:)`.
- `ServicesMenuRegistration` (`FinderUninstallService.swift:ServicesMenuRegistration`): `defaultsKey = "servicesRegisteredForBuild"`, `refreshIfNeeded(defaults:build:)`, `shouldRefresh(recorded:current:)`, and `currentBuild` (version plus build). It calls `NSUpdateDynamicServices()` once per build and records the build in user defaults.
- `ExternalUninstallRequest` (`ExternalUninstallRequest.swift:ExternalUninstallRequest`) is `nonisolated`; `Outcome` has `.uninstall(appID:name:)`, `.noApp`, `.severalApps(count:)`, `.outsideApplications(name:folder:)`, `.insideAnotherApp(name:)`, `.appleApp(name:)`, and `.purgeItself`. `refusal`, `refusalWithoutAddingFolder`, `resolve(urls:roots:purgeBundleIDs:readInfo:)`, `canOfferToAdd(folder:home:)`, `readInfoPlist(at:)`, and `displayName(for:info:)` define the decision and its user-facing text.

## Invariants

- **All three intents foreground the app.** `UninstallAppIntent.swift:openAppWhenRun`, `ScanForJunkIntent.swift:openAppWhenRun`, and `CleanSafeJunkIntent.swift:openAppWhenRun` are all `true`; the window is where progress, review, and confirmation are shown.
- **Uninstall never removes without confirmation.** `IntentRouter.swift:showUninstaller(appID:name:)` only prepares selection and asks `requestUninstallReview()`; the intent description states that nothing is removed until the person confirms in Purge.
- **Clean Safe Junk is the menu bar's Clean, not a general delete.** `IntentRouter.swift:cleanSafeJunk(reduceMotion:now:)` delegates to `store.cleanSafeItemsFromOverview(reduceMotion:)`; `CleanSafeJunkIntent.swift:description` states that only Safe items move to the Trash and Check First items are left alone. Cleaning re-checks every item against the safety rules.
- **The router is main-actor bound.** `IntentRouter.swift:IntentRouter` is `@MainActor`, as are every intent's `perform()` and the Finder alert/service callbacks. `IntentRouter.shared` captures the live `AppEnvironment.store` and production closures; tests can inject a store, onboarding answer, review action, and no-op refusal.
- **Onboarding gates every entry point.** `IntentRouter.swift:scanMac()`, `IntentRouter.swift:cleanSafeJunk(reduceMotion:now:)`, and `IntentRouter.swift:showUninstaller(appID:name:)` first test `onboardingDone()` and reveal the window without scanning or uninstalling when setup is incomplete; `onboardingDone` is wired to `FirstRunGate.hasCompletedOnboarding`.
- **Scans are joined, not duplicated.** `IntentRouter.swift:scanMac()` calls `store.requestScanIfNeeded(.cachesAndDevTools)` when a category scan is already running and otherwise `store.scanEverything()`; it waits for the queue to settle and for both `isScanningAll` and `isScanningProjects` to be false. Without session results for `.cachesAndDevTools` it returns `.stopped` rather than claiming zero.
- **Clean uses only fresh-enough results.** `IntentRouter.swift:hasFreshCacheResults(now:)` requires session results, `store.lastScanCompletedAt`, and age within `MenuViewModel.stalenessWindow`; otherwise `cleanSafeJunk` rescans only `.cachesAndDevTools` via `store.scanThroughQueue(_:forced:)`. It re-checks `isDeleting` and `isInteractiveSafeCleanupInProgress` after waiting, and returns `.nothingToClean` when `safeRecoverableBytes` is zero or no clean starts.
- **Uninstall tolerates a stale app list.** `IntentRouter.swift:showUninstaller(appID:name:)` retries `store.requestScan(.apps)` once when the app exists on disk but is absent from the current list (#176). It re-reads the disk before opening the review, and reports `UninstallMiss.gone` or `.notListed` instead of opening an empty review. `IntentRouter.swift:isStillWaiting(on:)` requires the same selected tab, the same single selected app, and no `uninstallPlan`.
- **App identity is the bundle path.** `InstalledAppEntity.swift:init(_:iconPNG:)` sets `id` from `app.bundleURL.standardizedFileURL.path`; `IntentRouter.swift:showUninstaller(appID:name:)` uses that same string in `store.selectedAppIDs`, so an entity drops directly into the uninstaller's selection.
- **Entity queries stay off the main thread and are cached.** `InstalledAppEntity.swift:InstalledAppQuery` marks `entities(for:)`, `entities(matching:)`, and `suggestedEntities()` `@concurrent`; `InstalledAppEntity.swift:InstalledAppIndex` is an `actor`, caches the list for 30 seconds, and caches icons separately by path.
- **Finder input is exactly one app bundle.** `ExternalUninstallRequest.swift:resolve(urls:roots:purgeBundleIDs:readInfo:)` keeps only file URLs whose path extension is `app`, ignores non-app items, rejects multiple apps with `.severalApps(count:)`, returns `.purgeItself` for a bundle ID in `purgeBundleIDs`, rejects `AppUninstallScanPolicy.isProtectedApp` matches (including symlinked system apps), and requires containment in `AppUninstallScanPolicy.installedAppRoots()` using `PathContainment.isInside(_:_:)`. `AppFolders.folderDepth(ofAppAtPath:roots:)` must also be non-nil, which excludes apps nested inside another app or package.
- **Offering to add a folder follows the same acceptance rule.** `ExternalUninstallRequest.swift:canOfferToAdd(folder:home:)` returns true only when `AppFolders.refusal(forPicked:home:otherRoots:)` is `nil`, so the alert never offers a folder that would then be refused (#99).
- **Finder routing is last-request-wins and main-actor bound.** `ExternalUninstallHandler.swift:handle(urls:)` increments `latestRequest`, resolves folder roots and `canOfferToAdd` in `Task.detached(priority: .userInitiated)`, and drops a result whose request number is no longer current. All alert and router work runs on the `@MainActor` handler.
- **The Finder service name and plist must stay in sync.** `FinderUninstallService.swift:menuTitle` and `FinderUninstallService.swift:message` are documented as the `NSMenuItem` title and `NSMessage`; the code notes a test checks they agree with `Info.plist`, so changing one without the other breaks the Services entry.
- **Services refresh is once per build and never in the test host.** `ServicesMenuRegistration.swift:refreshIfNeeded(defaults:build:)` returns early when `TestHost.isActive()`, then refreshes only when `shouldRefresh(recorded:current:)` says the stored build differs from `currentBuild` (version plus build).
- **Answers bold only an actual formatted size.** `IntentAnswer.swift:bolding(_:in:)` modifies only the range of `formatBytes(bytes)` when present; otherwise the answer is unchanged. Positive-size dialogs are the only ones passed through it.

## Side Effects

- **Window and app state:** `IntentRouter.revealWindow()` calls `RemovedAppMonitor.shared.userOpenedWindow()` and `AppWindowPresenter.reveal()`. `scanMac()` and `cleanSafeJunk(...)` set `store.selectedTab = .overview`; `showUninstaller(...)` sets `selectedTab`, `uninstallSection`, `selectedAppIDs`, and `uninstallerFocus`.
- **Scans and cleans:** `IntentRouter.scanMac()` calls `store.scanEverything()`, `store.requestScanIfNeeded(_:)`, `store.waitUntilSettled(_:)`, and reads `store.safeRecoverableBytes`. `IntentRouter.cleanSafeJunk(...)` may call `store.scanThroughQueue(_:forced:)`, then `store.cleanSafeItemsFromOverview(reduceMotion:)`, which moves Safe items to the Trash and shows the cleaning screen. `showUninstaller(...)` calls `store.refreshPermission()`, `store.requestScanIfNeeded(.apps)`, and `store.requestScan(.apps)`.
- **Alerts and app activation:** `ExternalUninstallHandler.showRefusal(title:detail:)` calls `NSApp.activate(ignoringOtherApps: true)` and runs a modal `NSAlert`. `ExternalUninstallHandler.offerToAddFolder(...)` does the same, then calls `AppFolderAdding.add(folderURL, store: AppEnvironment.store)` and re-runs `handle(urls:)` on success.
- **User defaults:** `ServicesMenuRegistration.refreshIfNeeded(...)` reads and writes `"servicesRegisteredForBuild"` in the supplied `UserDefaults` (`.standard` by default) after `NSUpdateDynamicServices()`. No other file in this directory accesses user defaults.
- **Shortcuts system state:** `PurgeAppShortcuts.refreshAppList()` calls `updateAppShortcutParameters()`, which re-reads the installed apps behind the "Uninstall <app>" phrases.
- **Disk and AppKit reads:** `InstalledAppIndex.apps(now:)` calls `AppUninstallScanner.discoverInstalledApps()`; `InstalledAppIndex.iconPNG(atPath:side:)` calls `NSWorkspace.shared.icon(forFile:)` and draws an icon bitmap. `ExternalUninstallRequest` reads each candidate's `Info.plist` through `AppBundleReach.readInfoPlist(at:)` and checks the filesystem with `FileManager.fileExists(atPath:)`.
- **Pasteboard:** `FinderUninstallService.uninstallWithPurge(_:userData:error:)` reads file URLs from the pasteboard supplied by the Services system.
- **No process launches or direct file deletion:** this directory starts no subprocesses and performs no deletion itself; cleaning and uninstall effects are performed by `PurgeStore` and the deletion engine.
